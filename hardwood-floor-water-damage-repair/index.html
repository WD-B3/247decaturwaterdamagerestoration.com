Hardwood floor water damage repair in Decatur starts with drying, because wood and laminate swell as they absorb water. Cupping means the edges rise above the center, crowning is the reverse, and buckling is when planks lift off the subfloor. What water does to a wood floor

Wood takes on water from above and from below. A spill on the surface soaks into seams, and a leak below, such as a slab, a crawl space or a pipe, pushes moisture up from the subfloor. Planks swell, and finish can whiten, stain or crack.

Engineered wood and laminate have layers or a fiberboard core that swells and does not return. Solid hardwood is more forgiving if caught early, but a floor that has been wet for days may be past drying alone.

How we dry hardwood floors

We measure moisture in the boards and in the subfloor, then use floor drying mats or panels with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. Mats pull moisture out through the board faces without lifting the floor. In some cases we remove a few boards or baseboard to create airflow.

Drying a hardwood floor can take longer than other materials, and the aim is to dry slowly enough to reduce cracking. Cupping often reduces as the floor dries, and we recommend waiting until readings stabilize before sanding.

Repair and refinishing

After drying, we may sand and refinish a floor that flattened, or replace boards that stayed warped. Matching the stain and finish across a room is a skilled job, and sometimes the whole room is refinished for a uniform look.

Do not sand or refinish while the floor is still holding moisture. It can trap water and cause new cupping. We confirm readings first.

Insurance and wood floors

Sudden leaks are often covered, while long-term seepage often is not. Policies differ on how they pay for flooring, so read yours or ask your insurer.

Photograph the floor and any visible source before drying. We provide a written scope with moisture readings.

Hardwood Floor Water Damage questions answered

Can a hardwood floor be saved after water damage?

Often, if it is dried quickly and the water is clean. Floors that have buckled or been wet for days may need board replacement.

How long does it take to dry a hardwood floor?

Several days to a couple of weeks is common, depending on wood, moisture and equipment. We check readings and do not guess.

Should I sand the floor right away?

No. Sanding while the wood is wet can trap moisture and cause new cupping. Wait for stable readings.

Does laminate flooring recover from water?

Usually not. Laminate swells at the edges and the core does not return. Affected areas typically get replaced.

Do you handle floor water emergencies at night?

Yes. Our line is answered 24/7 when water is active. Stop the source and call.
