Water damage restoration in Mooresville, AL has a different texture than a new subdivision. Incorporated in 1818, while Alabama was still a territory, the whole town is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Our emergency line is answered 24/7, and crews cross the Tennessee River from Decatur when dispatched.

This page is for Mooresville, Alabama, in Limestone County, not the Mooresville in North Carolina or Indiana. If your home is in ZIP 35649, you are in the right place.

Old walls, old materials: housing era first

Mooresville has a Brick Church from 1839, built of handmade brick, and a post office from 1840 at Lauderdale and High streets that is described as the oldest in operation in Alabama. The Stagecoach Inn and Tavern, a frame building put up before 1825, was restored in the 1990s. Buildings like these were not built to modern standards.

That matters after a leak. Handmade brick absorbs water and releases it slowly, old wood framing can hold moisture for weeks, and plaster does not behave like drywall. Drying such a building takes patience and readings, not just more fans. See our page on structural drying for the method.

When water is spreading in Mooresville

If water is moving through an older home now, quick steps limit what soaks into original materials.

Stop the water

Close the shut-off valve on the leaking line, or the main. Old valves can seize, so do not force them.

Cut power only if dry

Turn off wet-room breakers only when the panel can be reached without stepping in water. Old wiring and water are a bad mix.

Protect original materials

Lift rugs, books and wood furniture off wet floors. Do not scrub or sand wet original wood.

Call us before you tear out

Original plaster and woodwork may be salvageable. Ask before removing anything.

Historic status and repairs

Because the entire town is on the National Register, ask the town and the Alabama Historical Commission whether exterior repairs need review before you replace materials. A listing does not automatically control private repairs, but local rules and funding programs may apply.

Mooresville is in Limestone County, not Morgan County, so do not assume the City of Decatur Building Department handles your permit. Ask the town or county first. We document readings and removals so repairs can be matched to what was there.

Insurance and older homes

Sudden water damage is often covered by homeowners policies, but older homes sometimes have special coverage terms. Read your policy or ask your insurer, especially about replacement versus actual cash value for original materials.
