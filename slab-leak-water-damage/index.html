Slab leak water damage cleanup in Decatur applies to homes built on a concrete slab, where supply or drain lines run beneath the floor. When one of those lines fails, water rises through cracks and joints and soaks the flooring above. Our line is answered 24/7, and we respond the same day if crews can reach you.

Not every home in Decatur sits on a slab. Some have crawl spaces or basements, and the page on crawl space water damage covers those. This page helps if you think your leak is under a concrete floor.

Slab leak emergency signs that need a call now

A slab leak rarely announces itself with a flood. The signs are subtle and often show up in the bill or the flooring before anyone sees water.

  • A water bill that rises with no change in how you use water.
  • A warm area on the floor, which can point to a leaking hot water line.
  • The sound of running water when every fixture is off.
  • Flooring that lifts, buckles or feels damp, and baseboards that stain from below.
  • A musty smell that does not clear after cleaning.

What to do if you suspect a slab leak

Turn off all water fixtures and check the meter. If the dial or the low-flow indicator keeps moving, water is flowing somewhere. Close the main shut-off valve to stop the water until a plumber finds the leak.

A plumber uses electronic leak detection to locate the line. We cannot repair the line, and we do not break the slab. After the leak is found and repaired, we dry the flooring and structure above it. Never lift flooring and leave it wet. Water under a laminate or wood floor needs drying in place.

Why we treat it as a hidden-water job

Water under a slab spreads along the bottom of the concrete and comes up wherever it finds a crack, a control joint or a wall plate. By the time it shows, it may have soaked the bottom plates of interior walls and the flooring in several rooms.

We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map where it has traveled, then remove damaged materials, such as soaked baseboards and floating floors, and dry the rest with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. Compare this with the process on our structural drying page, which describes the equipment.

Slab leak, insurance and the plumbing repair

Policies treat slab leaks differently. Some cover resulting water damage, and some exclude damage from long-term leakage or limit what they pay for tearing out the slab. The line itself is usually a maintenance cost, not a covered loss. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

Keep the plumber leak detection report and the invoice. We provide a written scope of the damage and drying readings.

Reading your meter in Decatur

Decatur Utilities provides your water and takes billing through its SmartHub portal. A sudden rise in usage is often the first clue to a hidden leak, so compare recent bills before you assume the cause is seasonal.

The City of Decatur Building Department treats repair and remodel work on existing structures as permit work. If a plumber has to break the slab, ask them whether a permit applies. Then keep your receipts, because they support any claim.

Slab Leak Water Damage questions answered

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. Dispatch is answered 24/7. If you hear running water or see wet flooring, close the main valve and call.

Can you find a slab leak?

A plumber locates it with leak detection equipment. We handle the water damage once it is found and repaired.

Do all Decatur homes have slabs?

No. Some have crawl spaces or basements. Slab leaks only apply to homes with concrete floors on the ground, and we do not guess how common they are in a given neighborhood.

Will insurance cover a slab leak?

Policies vary. Some cover sudden water damage but exclude long-term leakage or limit coverage for repairing the line. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

How do I know the leak is under the floor?

If water bills climb, the meter moves with everything off and no visible leak is found, a plumber may test for a slab leak. A warm floor spot is another sign.
