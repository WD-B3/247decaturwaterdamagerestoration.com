What to do right away

Close the valve

Shut the water at the fixture or the main. A burst line can empty into a house quickly, so do this first.

Check the power

Turn off breakers to wet rooms only if you can reach the panel on dry ground. If not, stay out and call the electric utility.

Avoid sewage

If the water came up from a drain, keep people and pets away and do not run the washer or dishwasher.

Document it

Photograph the damage before cleanup and keep receipts.
