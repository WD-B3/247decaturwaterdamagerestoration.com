What to do in the first hour

Find the source

Shut the valve at the fixture or the main. If you cannot find the source, leave the main closed and call.

Protect your footing

Do not walk on a sagging ceiling or through standing water near outlets. Water and electricity are a dangerous mix.

Lift small items

Move rugs, books and electronics to a dry room. Leave heavy furniture where it is if moving it risks injury.

Keep records

Photograph the damage and keep receipts for anything you buy. Your insurer will want both.

Why Albany homes need a careful look

Many Albany houses are older, and older houses hide water. A stain on a ceiling can be a pipe failure two rooms away. A soft floor can be a leak under the house. We trace the wet area with a moisture meter before we decide what to dry and what to remove.

Water that came from a toilet overflow or a sewer backup is a different job. It can carry contaminants, so keep children and pets out and read our page on sewage backup cleanup.

Historic district notes

The City of Decatur reviews exterior design changes in the Albany and Old Decatur historic districts through its Architectural Review Board. If water damage means new siding, windows or roofing, ask city planning before you order materials.

Interior drying is a separate matter, and rules can differ, so confirm with the city. We do not issue permits or approvals, and we do not guarantee that a repair will be approved.

Insurance, flood zones and mold

Sudden pipe failures are the kind of loss many homeowners policies address, and flood damage is often excluded. Policies differ, so ask your insurer. We cannot promise coverage. We give you photos and a moisture log to hand over.
