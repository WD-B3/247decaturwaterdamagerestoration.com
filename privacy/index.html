Privacy Policy

What we collect when you call or email, and what we do with it.

This policy explains how Decatur Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this website, call our line or email us. Effective November 10, 2026.

Information we collect

This website does not use contact forms. We collect information only when you choose to give it to us, mainly by phone or email. That may include your name, phone number, address, a description of the water damage and details about your insurance.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call, you agree to this. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request and dispatch a crew.
  • To prepare a scope and explain the price.
  • To keep records of work performed.
  • To improve our service and train staff.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share details with insurers or other parties you ask us to work with, with service providers who help us operate, and where the law requires it.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds Google Maps. Those providers may collect technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads them. Their policies govern that data.

Retention and security

We keep job records for as long as needed for business, legal and insurance reasons. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and record-keeping duties. Contact us at contact@decaturwaterdamagepros.com or call (256) 667-9078.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date on this page.
