Call for water damage help

Call (256) 667-9078 at any hour. Tell the dispatcher what happened, where the water came from and whether it is still running. We offer same-day response when available, and we explain the scope and price before work starts.

We do not use contact forms. A call is faster, and a person can ask you the questions that decide what equipment to bring.

Before you call

  • If it is safe, shut off the water at the fixture or the main valve.
  • Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  • Keep people and pets out of contaminated water.
  • Take photos of the damage before you move things.
  • Have your address and a short description ready.
