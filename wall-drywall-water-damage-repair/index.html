Wall and drywall water damage repair in Decatur deals with the surfaces and cavities inside your walls. Drywall acts like a sponge, wicking water upward from the bottom, and the insulation and framing behind it hold moisture long after the paint looks fine. Our line is answered 24/7 for active leaks, and we respond the same day if crews can reach you.

Ceilings are covered under ceiling water damage repair, and the water source pages include burst pipes. This page is about the wall material and what repairs it.

Signs of water damage in a wall

A wall that has taken on water shows it in several ways. Not all are visible, which is why a moisture meter matters.

  • Stains, tide lines or yellowing on the paint.
  • Bubbling, cracking or peeling paint and wallpaper.
  • Soft, crumbling or sagging drywall.
  • Baseboards that swell, separate or stain.
  • A musty smell, or a damp spot that returns after painting.
  • Wall corners that feel cool or damp to the touch.

How far water travels in a wall

Drywall wicks water upward, so a puddle at the bottom can raise a damp line a foot or more above the floor. Water also runs down inside the cavity and wets insulation and the bottom plate. A supply line failure inside the wall can wet both sides and run into the next room.

We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to mark the wet area before cutting. The cut line usually goes a few inches past the last wet reading, and we check the other side of the wall.

Dry, cut or replace

Drywall that has been wet with clean water for a short time can sometimes be dried in place. Drywall that stays wet, sags, crumbles or has been wet with contaminated water is cut out. We remove the wet section, dry the cavity, then hang, tape, texture and paint.

Insulation that has been soaked is removed. Baseboards and trim are saved where possible.

Plaster walls and older homes

Plaster over wood lath was common in older homes, and it dries differently from drywall. Water can travel along lath, and plaster can loosen from its keys. A moisture meter is more important here because stains can appear far from the source.

Where plaster is damaged, the repair may need a plasterer. Ask the crew about your options.

Insurance and wall damage

Sudden failures are often covered, while long-term leaks and neglected maintenance often are not. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

Photograph walls before cutting and keep receipts. We provide a written scope listing removed areas and moisture readings.

Permits, licensing and the $10,000 line

The City of Decatur Building Department issues construction permits and lists repairs and remodeling of existing structures among the work that needs one. Ask the department whether your repair does, since rules can depend on scope.

The Alabama Home Builders Licensure Board defines a residential home builder as someone who performs repair or remodeling of a residential structure where the total cost exceeds $10,000. Ask any contractor about licensing, and the board has a public license lookup. Our drying work is covered under structural drying.

Wall and Drywall Water Damage questions answered

Can I paint over a water stain on my wall?

Only after the leak is fixed and the wall is dry. Paint over a damp wall traps moisture and the stain usually returns.

How do you know how much drywall to cut?

We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find the wet edge, then cut a few inches beyond it.

Do you handle wall leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. Dispatch is answered 24/7 for active leaks. Close the main valve first and then call.

Will insurance cover wall water damage?

Often when sudden. Not always for slow leaks. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

Do I need a permit to repair drywall?

The Building Department lists repairs and remodeling among work that needs a permit, but rules depend on scope. Ask the department.
