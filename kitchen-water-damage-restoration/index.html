Signs of water damage in a kitchen

Kitchen damage often hides inside cabinets and behind kick plates. Look for these signs.

  • Swollen, soft or warped cabinet bases and doors.
  • Stains or a musty smell in the cabinet under the sink.
  • Flooring that lifts, cups or feels soft near the sink or dishwasher.
  • Peeling paint, bubbles or stains on the wall behind appliances.
  • Countertop edges that swell or separate near the sink.

How we dry and repair a wet kitchen

We extract standing water and check cabinets, flooring and walls with moisture meters. Thermal imaging helps find water that has moved behind kick plates. Solid wood cabinets can sometimes be dried in place by removing toe kicks and drawers, and particleboard bases that swell are usually replaced.

We use air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, and often drill small access holes or remove baseboard to dry wall cavities. The kitchen floor, whether tile, vinyl, laminate or wood, is checked from above and below. For wood floors, see hardwood floor repair.

Insurance and kitchen leaks

Sudden failures such as a burst supply line or a dishwasher hose are often covered. Slow leaks under a sink that went on for months often are not. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

Photograph the cabinet interiors and the floor before demolition. We provide a written scope that lists what was removed and why.

Cabinets, toe kicks and what hides behind them

Kitchen water travels under cabinets and into the toe kick, where it stays out of sight. The base cabinet may look fine while the subfloor beneath it is wet. A moisture meter on the cabinet floor, the wall behind it and the flooring edge shows how far the water went.

Particleboard cabinets swell when wet and rarely return to shape. Solid wood and plywood boxes often dry in place. Which one you have changes the repair, so tell the dispatcher what the cabinets are made of if you know.

Water and drains in Decatur kitchens

Decatur Utilities provides your water, and the shut-off for the kitchen is usually under the sink. Find it and test it. If you do not have a local shut-off, the main valve at the house or the meter works as a backup.

A slow drain that keeps coming back may be a public line problem, and the utility takes calls about it. If the kitchen drain backs up with contaminated water, see our sewage backup page.

Kitchen Water Damage questions answered

Can swollen kitchen cabinets be saved?

Sometimes. Solid wood cabinets can often be dried if caught quickly. Particleboard cabinet bases that have swollen usually cannot be saved.

How do I know if water is under my kitchen floor?

Soft spots, cupping, a musty smell and stains near the sink or dishwasher are clues. A moisture meter confirms it.

Do I need to remove cabinets to dry the wall behind them?

Sometimes. We try drilling small access holes or removing toe kicks first. If readings stay high, the cabinet may need to come off.

Will insurance cover a kitchen leak?

Often if sudden and accidental. Not always for slow leaks. Read your policy or ask your insurer.
