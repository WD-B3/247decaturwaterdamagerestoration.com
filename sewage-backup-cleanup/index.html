Sewage backup cleanup in Decatur deals with Category 3 water, the most contaminated class under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. When a sewer line backs up through a floor drain, toilet or tub, the water carries bacteria and waste. Our line is answered 24/7, and crews respond on the same day if a crew is free.

If the source is a broken line inside the house, see burst pipe damage. If the backup came with flooding, the flood damage page covers that side.

Sewage backup emergency: what to do first

Stay out of the affected area, and keep children and pets out. Do not flush toilets or run sinks, tubs and washers, because more water adds to the backup. Do not try to clean it with household vacuums or mops.

Stay out and keep others out

Contaminated water can carry disease. Avoid skin contact and keep everyone else away.

Stop using water

Do not run sinks, showers, toilets or washers until the line is cleared.

Cut power only if dry

Turn off power to the affected area only if you can reach the panel on dry ground. Otherwise call the electric utility before anything else.

Call your utility if needed

If the backup could be in the public sewer, call Decatur Utilities. Then call a plumber if it is in your own line.

Where the backup starts

A clog in the private lateral between the house and the street is the plumber job. A blockage or overload in the public main is a utility job. A way to tell is whether other homes on the street are affected, but that is not reliable. A plumber with a camera can see the line.

Decatur Utilities provides drinking water and wastewater service in the city, and its main line takes calls about slow drains. Tell the utility if you suspect the main. We handle the contaminated water and structure after the line is cleared.

How we handle contaminated water

We wear protective equipment and use extraction units to remove the water. Porous materials that touched sewage, such as carpet, pad, insulation and low drywall, are removed. Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned and treated with an antimicrobial.

We dry the area with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers and take moisture readings to confirm. Odor needs drying and removal of contaminated materials, not masking. Contents that were in the water are sorted into cleanable and non-salvageable items.

Insurance and sewage backup

Many policies exclude sewer and drain backup unless a water backup endorsement was added. Check your policy for this wording or ask your insurer, since it is a separate coverage in many cases.

Photograph the backup, keep receipts and note when it began. We provide a written scope listing removed materials and drying readings.

Who to call in Decatur

Decatur Utilities provides wastewater service within the city. If the backup could be in the public main, call the utility first, because a plumber cannot clear a public line. If your own lateral is blocked, a licensed plumber clears it.

Community Development handles unsafe buildings in the city, and the Building Department issues permits for repairs of existing structures. Ask about permits before replacing flooring or walls after a backup.

Sewage Backup Cleanup questions answered

Do you handle sewage backups at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. Our line is answered 24/7 and crews dispatch on the same day if a crew is free. Stay out of the area and keep others away until we arrive.

Is sewage backup dangerous?

Yes. It is Category 3 water and can carry harmful microorganisms. Avoid contact and use professional cleanup. Ask your physician about health concerns.

Who clears the sewer line?

A licensed plumber clears a private lateral. Decatur Utilities handles the public main. We clean up the water and dry the structure.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewage backup?

Often only with an added endorsement. Read your policy for sewer and drain backup wording or ask your insurer.

Can sewage-damaged carpet be cleaned?

Usually not. Carpet and pad that touched sewage are normally removed. Hard surfaces can be cleaned and sanitized.
