Crawl space water damage cleanup in Decatur starts under the house, in the void between the ground and the floor. Water collects there from rain, a leaking line or a high water table, and it soaks insulation, joists and the subfloor. Our line is answered 24/7, and we send a crew on the same day if schedules allow.

This page covers the under-floor void only. A flooded finished lower level has its own page on basement water damage, and surface flooding is covered under flood damage cleanup.

Signs of crawl space water damage

Crawl space damage is easy to miss, because you have to look under the house. The signs show up inside first.

  • Floors that feel soft, bouncy or cold.
  • A musty smell that rises through vents and floor registers.
  • Sagging insulation or insulation lying on the ground.
  • Standing water or damp soil seen through the access hatch.
  • Rust on ductwork, nails or fasteners in the crawl space.
  • Cracked paint or door frames that stick after rain.

Where the water comes from

The source decides the fix. A leaking supply or drain line is a plumbing repair. Water that enters after rain is a drainage problem, and the repair may include grading, gutters, downspouts or a sump system. Vapor rising from bare soil is a moisture control problem and may need a vapor barrier.

We do not install drainage systems, and we do not do waterproofing. We remove the water, dry the structure and tell you plainly when the cause needs another trade.

How we dry a crawl space

We pump standing water, then remove wet insulation and any wet debris. Moisture meters check joists and the subfloor above. Dehumidifiers and air movers are placed to dry the cavity, and in some cases ducted to the access.

A crawl space dries slowly, since air is still and the ground keeps supplying moisture. We take readings over several visits and stop only when materials reach drying goals. Related moisture detection methods are on the structural drying page.

Insurance and crawl space water

Sudden pipe failures in a crawl space are often covered, while groundwater and seepage are often excluded. Flood damage is usually excluded as well. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

Photograph the standing water, the wet insulation and any visible source. A written scope from us lists what we pumped, removed and dried.

Wheeler Lake backwater and low ground

Flint Creek joins the Tennessee River at Decatur, and the water level in its lower end is affected by Wheeler Lake. When the lake is high, low ground near the creek can stay wet longer after rain, and a crawl space on such a lot can collect water even when the storm has passed.

South of the 14th Street and Magnolia Street area near US 31, the land is flat and drains slowly. Use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center to see your flood zone and ask the City of Decatur Building Department about floodplain rules before you rebuild a floor system.

Crawl Space Water Damage questions answered

Do you handle crawl space water emergencies at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. Our line is answered 24/7 and crews go out on the same day if schedules allow. A burst line under the house is especially urgent, so close the main valve first.

Can you waterproof a crawl space?

No. Waterproofing and drainage are separate trades. We remove the water and dry the space, and we point you to the right contractor when the cause is drainage.

Is mold common after crawl space water?

Wet wood and insulation in a still, humid space support growth. Prompt drying reduces the risk. For health questions, talk to your physician.

Should wet crawl space insulation be replaced?

Usually. Wet fiberglass loses its value and holds moisture against the floor system. We decide with moisture readings and condition.

How long does a crawl space take to dry?

It depends on the water amount and the materials. Several days is common, since air movement is limited and the ground can keep supplying moisture.
