Who we are

247 Decatur Water Damage Restoration is a water damage restoration business at 1605 Beltline Rd SW in Decatur, Alabama. We answer one line, around the clock, and we send crews to Decatur, Trinity, Mooresville, Tanner and Somerville.

We built the business around a simple idea. A homeowner with water on the floor needs three things fast: someone to answer, a plan they can understand and proof that the work was done. We try to deliver those three things on every call.

How we work

  • We answer the phone 24 hours a day, every day.
  • We explain the scope and price before work starts.
  • We respond on the same day if schedules allow. We do not promise a fixed arrival time.
  • We measure moisture and log the readings.
  • We tell you what can be dried and what must be removed.
  • We give you photos and records for your insurer.

What we do

Our work is water removal and structural drying. We extract standing water, remove materials that cannot be dried and run air movers and dehumidifiers until readings reach the target. The services page lists each situation in detail.

We stay inside our lane. We do not file insurance claims, we do not diagnose plumbing, and we do not offer mold services. When another trade is needed, such as a plumber for a failed pipe, we tell you.

What we will not promise

We will not promise that your insurer will pay. We will not promise an arrival time, because traffic, weather and call volume change. We will not quote a price before we know what is wet.

What we will promise is plain talk. If the damage is small, we will say so. If drying in place can save a floor, we will try it. If a material must go, we will show you the reading that says so.

Where we work

We serve the city of Decatur and nearby towns. The service area page lists each town with its ZIP code and links to a page with local notes.

The area sits on the Tennessee River, Wheeler Lake and Flint Creek, and water problems here follow the seasons. Our guide to seasonal risks explains what to watch for each time of year.

How to reach us

Call (256) 667-9078 at any hour. You can also email contact@247decaturwaterdamagerestoration.com, though for an active leak the phone is faster. Our address is 1605 Beltline Rd SW, Decatur, AL 35601.
