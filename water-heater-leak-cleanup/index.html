Water heater leaking now: safety first

A failing water heater can involve hot water, electricity or natural gas. Decatur Utilities supplies natural gas as well as water, and a gas smell is a reason to leave and call 911 or the gas utility before anything else.

Smell gas? Leave

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility from outside. Do not flip switches.

Turn off the heater

For an electric unit, turn off the breaker if the panel is dry. For gas, set the control to off.

Close the cold supply

Shut the cold water inlet valve above the tank to stop more water entering.

Keep clear of hot water

Water from a failed tank can be scalding. Keep people and pets out of the room.

How water heaters fail

Tanks corrode from the inside, and the sediment buildup that comes with hard water speeds it up. A pinhole in the tank shows as a slow drip, while a full rupture releases the contents at once. Fittings, the temperature and pressure relief valve and the drain valve are the other common leak points.

A slow leak is the harder one. It can soak the floor under the heater and travel into adjacent walls before anyone notices, and the damage looks like a water stain on the baseboard. We check moisture a few feet beyond the visible wet area.

How we clean up and dry

We extract water from the floor and use moisture meters to locate hidden wetness in adjacent walls, flooring and subfloor. Wet drywall and baseboards near the unit are removed when readings stay high.

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry what remains. If the unit sits in a finished space such as a hall closet, flooring and cabinetry need extra checking. For floor damage see hardwood floor repair. A licensed plumber handles the heater replacement.

Insurance and a water heater failure

A sudden tank failure is often treated as sudden and accidental damage, but the heater itself may not be covered since it is wear and tear. A slow leak that went on for months may be excluded. Ask your insurer how your policy treats it.

Keep the failed unit or the label with the model and age, along with photos of the leak and the damage.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup questions answered

Do you handle water heater emergencies at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. Dispatch is answered 24/7 and a crew goes out on the same day if a crew is free. Shut off the cold water supply to the tank before you call if you can reach it safely.

Do you replace water heaters?

No. A licensed plumber replaces or repairs the heater. We handle the water cleanup and drying afterward, and we coordinate timing with your plumber.

Is hot water from a water heater dangerous?

Yes. A ruptured tank can release scalding water. Keep people and pets out until it cools, and do not step in the water if the unit is electric and the panel is not off.

Can I tell how old a water heater is?

The label or serial number usually shows the manufacture date. Keep it with your claim notes, since insurers often ask about the unit age.

Will insurance cover a leaking water heater?

Insurers often cover the resulting water damage if the failure was sudden, but may not cover the heater itself. Policies vary, so read yours or ask your insurer.
