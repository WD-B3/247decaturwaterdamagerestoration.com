Carpet water damage restoration in Decatur depends on one question first: what kind of water was it. Clean water caught quickly can often be extracted and the carpet dried in place. Water that sat or came from a contaminated source usually means the carpet and pad are removed. Our line is answered 24/7, and we send a crew the same day whenever a crew can get there.

This page covers carpet and pad. Wood and laminate are on hardwood floor repair, and standing water is covered under water extraction.

Is the carpet salvageable?

Carpet fibers dry fast, but the pad underneath holds water and the subfloor beneath it holds more. Most of the damage is in the layers you cannot see. Three factors decide the outcome.

  • Water category. Clean water is the best case, while grey water and sewage mean removal.
  • Time. After about 48 hours the odds fall and mold risk rises.
  • Backing and pad type. Some pads can be dried, and others are replaced as standard.

How we dry carpet

We extract with a weighted wand that pulls water up through the pad. Then we lift the carpet in a corner and check the pad and subfloor with a moisture meter. If the carpet can stay, we use a floating technique, which lifts it from the pad so air can pass underneath.

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the layers, and we recheck until readings reach drying goals. When the pad has to go, we remove it and dry the subfloor before laying a new one.

Pad replacement and re-installation

If the carpet is salvageable but the pad is not, we can lift the carpet, remove the pad and put the carpet back over new pad after the floor is dry. Carpet that has been stretched many times may not reinstall well, and a seam can show.

For rental properties and multi-room losses, replacing the carpet is sometimes cleaner. Ask us what the readings show before you decide.

Insurance and carpet

Many policies cover carpet damaged by sudden water, and some pay on depreciated value. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

Photograph the carpet before extraction and keep a sample of removed material if your adjuster needs one.

Carpet backing and tack strips

Carpet sits on tack strips at the wall edges, and the strips can hold water against the baseboard long after the middle of the room feels dry. The crew lifts an edge to read moisture at the perimeter, not just the center.

Latex backing delaminates when soaked for days. If the backing peels or the carpet smells sour, replacement is usually the sounder choice.

Humid summers and wet carpet

North Alabama summers are humid, and a damp carpet does not dry well in warm, wet air. In the cooler months, carpet dries more easily, but you still need readings to know.

Carpet Water Damage questions answered

Can wet carpet be dried instead of replaced?

Sometimes. Clean water caught within a day or two can often be dried in place, but the pad is usually replaced. Water that came from a contaminated source is a removal job.

How fast does carpet need attention after water?

As quickly as possible. After 24 to 48 hours, odor and mold risk rise and the carpet is less likely to be saved.

Is the pad always replaced?

Often, because it acts like a sponge. We check it with a moisture meter and tell you.

Will wet carpet smell?

It can. Drying and removing damaged materials is the fix, not covering the smell.

Do you handle carpet water emergencies at night?

Yes. Our dispatch line is answered 24/7. Stop the water first and then call.
