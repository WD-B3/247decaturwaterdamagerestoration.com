Steps that limit the damage

Stop the leak

Turn off the water to the appliance, or close the main valve. Unplug small appliances only if the outlet is dry.

Mind the power

Do not touch outlets or cords in standing water. Switch off breakers for wet rooms only if you can reach the panel on dry ground.

Pull up what you can

Lift small rugs and loose items. Do not use a household vacuum on water.

Take photos

Photograph every wet room before you start. Keep receipts for anything you buy.
