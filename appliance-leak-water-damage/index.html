Appliance leak: what to do right now

If an appliance is leaking, you can stop most of it in under a minute. The shut-off valves sit on the wall behind or beside the unit.

Close the supply valve

Turn the valve behind the washer or dishwasher, or the line under the sink, to off.

Unplug if dry

Unplug the unit only if the outlet is dry. If water is at the outlet, cut the breaker or stay clear.

Pull the unit out safely

Slide the appliance forward to see where the water is going. Do not drag it through standing water.

Photograph before cleanup

Capture the hose, the valve and the wet floor for your claim.

Where appliance leaks come from

Rubber washer hoses age and burst, while braided steel hoses last longer but can still fail at the fitting. Dishwasher leaks come from door gaskets, drain hoses and the inlet valve. Refrigerator leaks usually come from the icemaker line or the defrost drain, and both can run for weeks without a puddle on the visible floor.

A slow leak is the costly one. Water works under the flooring and into the subfloor, and the first sign may be a soft spot or a musty smell rather than a puddle. Our moisture meter checks help find where the water has gone.

How we find and dry it

We extract standing water first and check flooring, baseboards and cabinet bases with moisture meters. Thermal imaging helps trace water that has moved sideways under tile or laminate.

Materials that cannot be dried, such as swollen particleboard cabinet bases or soaked underlayment, are removed. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the rest, and we document readings each visit. If the floor is wood, see hardwood floor water damage repair for how cupping is handled.

Insurance and appliance leaks

Many policies cover water damage from a sudden appliance failure, but not the appliance itself, and they often exclude damage from a leak that went on for a long time. Read the policy language on sudden versus gradual damage, or ask your insurer.

Keep the failed hose or part, the model number and the date you found the leak. A written scope from us lists what was wet and what was removed.

What affects the cost

Duration of the leak

Appliance Leak Water Damage questions answered

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. Our line is answered 24/7 and crews go out on the same day if a crew is free. Close the supply valve before calling if you can reach it.

How do I know my dishwasher is leaking under the floor?

Look for a soft or cupped spot near the dishwasher, a musty smell or a stain on the cabinet base. A moisture meter can confirm what the surface hides.

Does insurance cover a washing machine hose burst?

Often the resulting water damage is covered when it was sudden, but the hose itself usually is not. Confirm the wording with your insurer.

Should I replace rubber washer hoses?

Many plumbers suggest braided steel hoses and checking them periodically. Ask a licensed plumber what fits your machine.

Can wet cabinets be saved?

Solid wood cabinets sometimes can. Particleboard cabinet bases that swell usually cannot, and we decide with moisture readings.
