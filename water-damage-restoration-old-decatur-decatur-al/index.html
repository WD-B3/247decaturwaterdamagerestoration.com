First steps when water is in an older home

Stop the water

Close the shutoff at the fixture or the main valve. In an older house the main may be stiff, so find it before you need it.

Be careful with power

Switch off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you can reach it standing on dry ground. Otherwise stay out and call the electric utility.

Leave old finishes alone

Do not scrape, sand or tear out old paint, plaster or flooring on your own. Homes of this age can contain lead paint or asbestos materials, so get advice before disturbing them.

Photograph first

Take photos and video of the damage before anything moves. Your insurer will ask for them.

Historic district rules for repairs

The City of Decatur uses an Architectural Review Board to review exterior design changes in the Old Decatur and Albany historic districts. Drying wet interior materials is one question. Replacing siding, windows, porch parts or roofing is another.

Ask the city planning office before you plan exterior work, and tell your insurer what you intend to replace. Rules for interior work can differ from exterior work, so confirm with the city instead of assuming. We do not issue permits or approvals.

Insurance, flood zones and mold

Homeowners policies often cover sudden events such as a burst line and often exclude slow seepage and flood. Policies differ, so read yours or ask your insurer before you decide. We cannot promise that any claim will be paid. We provide photos and moisture records you can pass along.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Look up your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center to see its mapped zone.

Damp materials can grow mold within a day or two, which is why fast drying matters. We dry structures. We do not offer mold testing or removal, and we will say so if you need a separate specialist.

Questions from Old Decatur homeowners

Do you serve Old Decatur?

Yes. Our line is answered 24 hours a day, and we send a crew from Decatur the same day if crews can reach you. We never promise a fixed arrival time.

Do I need approval to repair water damage in a historic district?

Exterior changes in the Old Decatur and Albany historic districts go through the city's Architectural Review Board. Ask city planning before replacing exterior materials. We do not issue approvals.

Can original hardwood floors be saved?

Sometimes. It depends on how long the water sat and how wet the subfloor is. Moisture readings tell us whether drying in place is realistic.

Is it safe to remove old plaster or paint myself?

Not without advice. Older homes can contain lead paint or asbestos materials. Ask a qualified tester before sanding, scraping or demolishing them.
