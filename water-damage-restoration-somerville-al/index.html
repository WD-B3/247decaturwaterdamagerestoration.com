Water damage restoration in Somerville, AL serves a small Morgan County town about 15 miles southeast of Decatur. Somerville was the county seat until 1891, and State Highway 67 runs through it. The emergency line answers at all hours, and a crew heads out from Decatur that same day when a crew is open.

This page is for Somerville, Alabama, in Morgan County, ZIP 35670, not the Somerville in Tennessee or Massachusetts.

Around the old courthouse: neighborhood first

The 1837 Morgan County Courthouse in Somerville is on the National Register of Historic Places and is described as the oldest intact courthouse in Alabama. The Rice-Green-Pryor House, from the 1830s, is also listed, and Morrow Cemetery and the Old Cotaco White House are on the Alabama Register of Landmarks and Heritage.

The area around the old courthouse holds some of the town's oldest listed buildings. Brick and old wood framing hold moisture longer than new construction, so a leak there needs patient drying. See structural drying and wall and drywall repair.

When water is spreading in Somerville

If water is spreading in your home, these steps help right away.

Stop the source

Shut the valve at the leaking fixture, or close the main where the line enters the house.

Power off only if dry

Kill the breakers for wet rooms, but only if you can get to the panel without standing in water.

Lift valuables

Move furniture and electronics out of water. Keep paper and books up high.

Call from a dry place

Tell us what happened and whether water is still coming in.

Calling from Somerville

Give the dispatcher the street, the nearest crossroad and what happened. If you are near the courthouse square, say so. Rural roads outside town can be hard to find after dark, and a landmark saves minutes.

We send a crew from Decatur. Travel time depends on traffic and call volume, so we offer same-day response when available and do not promise a fixed arrival time.

What to do while you wait

  • Shut off water at the fixture or the main valve if it is safe.
  • Move small furniture and rugs off wet floors.
  • Take photos of the damage before cleanup.
  • Keep people and pets out of standing water.
  • Do not open walls or ceilings before the crew takes readings.

Jurisdiction and permits

Somerville is in Morgan County, like Decatur, but it is its own town. Ask Somerville town hall or Morgan County about permits for repair work rather than assuming the City of Decatur Building Department handles them.

For your water service, ask your provider, since utilities vary by address. We document what we removed so permits and inspections go smoothly.

Insurance and your policy

Insurers tend to pay for sudden events such as a split supply line and to resist long-running seepage. Rising flood water is generally excluded. Your own policy wording settles it, so call your insurer.

Photograph damage before cleanup. We give you a written scope.
