A burst pipe in a Decatur home is an emergency from the first minute, because a pressurized supply line can pour water into walls, ceilings and floors until it is shut off. Our dispatch line is answered 24/7, and we respond the same day if crews can reach you.

This page covers the damage that follows a failed supply line, from the shut-off to the drying. Frozen lines have their own page on frozen pipe damage, and a leak that sits under a concrete floor is covered under slab leaks.

Burst pipe emergency: what to do in the first ten minutes

Speed matters more than anything else you do. The water is clean when it starts, but it picks up dirt and material as it travels, and every minute spreads it further.

Close the main valve

Turn the main shut-off valve to closed. In many Decatur homes it is where the water line enters the house, and the meter shut-off at the street is the backup.

Open the lowest faucet

Opening a faucet at the lowest level drains pressure and the water left in the lines.

Power down wet areas

Turn off breakers to wet rooms only if the panel is dry to reach. Never touch a switch or cord standing in water.

Photograph before moving

Take quick photos and video of the failure and the wet areas. They support an insurance claim later.

Why supply lines burst

Supply pipes fail for several reasons that have nothing to do with weather. Corroded copper develops pinholes, fittings and push connectors loosen over years, and old galvanized steel rusts from the inside. High water pressure and a failed pressure reducing valve can split a line that was fine for decades.

The location tells you a lot. A burst line in an exterior wall or an unheated space often points to cold. A failure inside an interior wall, in a ceiling, or at a fitting under a sink is usually age or pressure. We note this in the written scope because it affects what a plumber has to repair.

How we handle the damage

After extraction we open the affected walls and ceilings where moisture meters and thermal imaging show water. Drywall that stayed wet is cut out with a margin, wet insulation comes out, and the cavity is dried with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers.

We repair the structure after it is dry, but we do not repair the pipe itself. A licensed plumber fixes the line, and we coordinate the order so drying equipment is not set up under an open leak. Related work such as drywall repair and kitchen cabinet drying follows the same plan.

Insurance and a burst pipe

Sudden and accidental pipe bursts are the kind of loss many homeowners policies are written to cover, while a slow leak that went unnoticed for months often is not. Policies differ on what they pay for the pipe itself versus the damage it caused, so read the wording or ask your insurer.

Keep the failed section of pipe if you can, along with photos, receipts and notes on when you shut the water off. A clear written scope from us gives your adjuster a specific document to review.

What affects the cost of burst pipe cleanup

How long it ran

A line caught in minutes soaks one room. One that ran overnight can reach several rooms and the level below.

Where it burst

A pipe in a ceiling cavity wets insulation, drywall and whatever is below. A pipe in a slab-level wall is different.

Materials soaked

Drywall, insulation, flooring and cabinetry each have their own removal and drying needs.

Drying time

Days of air movers and dehumidifiers add up, and thick materials dry more slowly.

Rebuild scope

Replacing drywall, paint and flooring after drying is priced apart from the mitigation.

Shutting off water in a Decatur home

Decatur Utilities provides drinking water and wastewater service in the city, and a service meter sits between the street and your house. Find your main shut-off before you need it, and check that it turns. A valve that has not moved in ten years can seize.

If you cannot close the main inside the house, call Decatur Utilities and ask about the meter shut-off. After any repair that needs a permit, ask the City of Decatur Building Department whether it applies, because its page lists repairs and remodeling of existing structures among the work that needs one.

Burst Pipe Water Damage questions answered

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, including holidays. A crew is dispatched the same day if crews can reach you. Call, describe where the water is coming from and tell us whether you have shut it off.

Do you fix the pipe itself?

No. A licensed plumber repairs or replaces the line. We remove water, dry the structure and repair the damage it caused, and we coordinate timing with your plumber.

How much water can a burst pipe release?

It depends on pipe size and pressure, but a failed supply line can release many gallons a minute. A few hours of running water is enough to soak walls, ceilings and the floor below, which is why shutting the valve first matters.

Will my homeowners insurance cover a burst pipe?

Sudden and accidental bursts are often covered, but policies differ. Gradual leaks and neglect are often excluded. Read your policy or ask your insurer, and document everything before cleanup begins.

Can I dry the walls myself with fans?

Household fans move surface air but do not pull moisture out of wall cavities. Wet insulation and drywall usually need to be opened and dried with air movers and dehumidifiers. We check with a moisture meter first.
