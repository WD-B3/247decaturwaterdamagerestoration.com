AC leak water damage repair in Decatur deals with the water that a cooling system leaves behind. An air conditioner pulls humidity out of the air, and that water has to drain. When a drain line clogs or a pan overflows, the water goes into ceilings, closets and floors. Our line is open 24/7 if the leak is active.

This page is about the damage, not the unit. An HVAC technician repairs the system. If water has already stained your ceiling, our page on ceiling water damage repair covers the repair side.

How an AC leak happens

Algae and debris clog the condensate drain over a long humid summer, and the water backs up into the drain pan. Rusted pans, cracked secondary pans and loose connections let it escape. A frozen evaporator coil that thaws can overflow a pan, and a dirty filter can contribute by restricting airflow.

Because this water is often clean but slightly dirty with biofilm, the first sign may be a stain near a vent or a closet floor that stays damp. Attic units can leak above ceilings, and closet units can leak into walls and floors.

What to do when the AC is leaking

Turn off the thermostat and the air handler at the breaker to stop it running and making more water. If water is dripping, place a bucket and keep the area clear.

Do not remove the drain line cap and pour bleach without checking with an HVAC technician. Photograph the pan, the stain and any standing water, and call an HVAC technician to fix the unit. Call us when the water has reached floors, walls or the ceiling.

How we dry and repair

We locate moisture with meters and thermal imaging. If insulation in an attic is wet, it is removed and replaced, and ceiling drywall that has stayed damp is cut out and dried. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry subfloor and framing.

AC leaks are slow and often hidden. If a leak ran for weeks, we also check for surface growth and discuss what needs replacing. For the broader drying method see structural drying.

Insurance and an AC leak

Sudden pan overflows may be covered while a long-term leak from poor maintenance may not. Read your policy or ask your insurer. Keep HVAC service records, since they show maintenance history.

A written scope from us documents what was wet and what we removed.

Humidity and AC in the Tennessee Valley

Summers in north Alabama are humid, which means air conditioners run hard and produce a lot of condensate. That raises the odds of a clogged drain line over a long cooling season, and it makes drying slower because the outdoor air is wet too.

The City of Decatur Building Department lists installation of HVAC units among the work that requires a permit. If your repair involves replacing the unit, ask the contractor about the permit. For lower levels see basement water damage.

AC Leak Water Damage questions answered

Do you handle AC leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. Our dispatch line is answered 24/7. If water is actively dripping, turn off the system first and then call.

Do you repair air conditioners?

No. An HVAC technician repairs the unit and clears the drain. We dry and repair the structure afterward.

Why is my AC leaking when it is not very hot?

A clogged condensate line, a dirty filter or a frozen coil can cause water to back up even in mild weather. A technician can diagnose it.

Can an AC leak cause mold?

Moisture that stays for a day or two can support growth. Prompt drying reduces that risk. For health questions, ask your physician.

How can I prevent AC leaks?

Change filters on schedule and ask an HVAC technician about drain line cleaning. A water sensor or float switch in the drain pan can shut the system off if water builds up.
