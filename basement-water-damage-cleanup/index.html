Basement water: when it is an emergency

A basement with standing water is an emergency, because the water soaks finishes and furniture and may reach the electrical panel or furnace.

Water near the panel or outlets is a shock risk. Call the electric utility if you are not sure.

Stop the source if you can

Close the supply valve if a line failed. Check the sump pump and its discharge line.

Where basement water comes from

A plumbing failure is the simplest case. Seepage through walls or the floor after heavy rain is a drainage matter. A sump pump that fails, loses power or is overwhelmed lets groundwater rise. A floor drain backup is a sewage matter, which is covered under sewage backup cleanup.

Restoration is not waterproofing. We remove water and dry the space, but sealing a wall, regrading a yard or adding drainage is for a different contractor. We tell you when the cause points that way.

How we clean and dry a basement

We pump standing water, then extract what is left and remove materials that cannot be saved. Carpet, pad, wet insulation and low drywall are common removals. Concrete and masonry are dried with dehumidifiers, which pull moisture from the air and the slab.

Below-grade rooms need steady, long drying. Concrete holds water and releases it slowly, so we monitor readings and run equipment until the surfaces reach drying goals. Related drying methods are on the structural drying page.

Insurance and basement water

Sudden plumbing failures are often covered, while seepage and groundwater are often excluded. Sump pump backup may need an added endorsement. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

Photograph the water level, the sump pump and any visible source. A written scope from us documents the work.

Hills, flat ground and basements in Decatur

Decatur is built on a short hill that drops steeply to the Tennessee River at Rhodes Ferry Park. The hill runs about 1.5 miles south to the 14th Street and Magnolia Street area, and the ground south of there is flat. Water on a slope moves toward the lowest lot, and flat ground can hold it.

Dry Branch and Flint Creek are the two named tributaries in the city, and Flint Creek is affected by Wheeler Lake levels. If your basement takes water after rain, check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address, and ask the City of Decatur Building Department about floodplain requirements.

Basement Water Damage questions answered

Do you handle basement flooding at night or on weekends in Decatur?

Yes. Our line is answered 24/7. Tell us the water level and whether it is still rising.

Can you stop my basement from leaking again?

No. We clean and dry. A waterproofing or drainage contractor addresses the cause of repeated seepage.

Is a wet basement dangerous?

It can be, if water reaches electrical panels, outlets or appliances. Stay out of deep water and call the electric utility if you are unsure.

How long does a basement take to dry?

Often several days, since concrete holds moisture. We check readings and stop when they reach drying goals.

Does insurance cover a flooded basement?

It depends on the cause. Sudden plumbing failures are often covered. Groundwater seepage and flood are often excluded. Read your policy or ask your insurer.
